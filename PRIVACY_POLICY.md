# Privacy Policy — Zameen Converter

**Effective Date:** October 11, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 11, 2026

**Zameen Converter** ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to respecting and protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning information collection, use, and disclosure.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
Zameen Converter operates as a fully offline utility application.
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email, phone number, or identity.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not request or track your GPS location. You select your state or region manually.
- **No Contact or Media Access:** We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, or personal files.
- **On-Device Storage Only:** Your conversion history, favorite items, selected region, and preferences are stored strictly locally on your device (using local device storage / localStorage). This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.

---

### 2. Advertising & Analytics (AdMob)
To keep the app free and maintained, the app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.
- Google AdMob may use standard device advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID or IDFA on iOS) and anonymous diagnostic metrics in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
- For information on how Google manages advertising data, visit: [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

---

### 3. Legal & Statutory Land Disclaimer
Calculations provided by Zameen Converter are computed based on published state land revenue standards and customary land metrics. Because local patwari, revenue tehsil, and settlement records may have specific customary definitions, users are strongly advised to verify all land measurements with their local revenue department or patwari before executing financial or legal transactions.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
